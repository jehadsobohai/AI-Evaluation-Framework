# Example Evaluations

This document provides brief examples of how the AI Evaluation Framework can be applied across different domains.

## LLM Evaluation

**Objective:** Compare two AI responses for instruction following and helpfulness.

**Result:** Response A was preferred because it followed the user's instructions more accurately and provided a more complete answer.

---

## Translation Evaluation

**Objective:** Compare two English-to-Arabic translations.

**Result:** Translation A was preferred because it preserved the original meaning, used more natural Arabic, and maintained consistent terminology.

---

## Medical AI Evaluation

**Objective:** Compare two medical responses for patient safety.

**Result:** Response A was preferred because it provided evidence-based advice, recommended appropriate medical follow-up, and identified emergency warning signs.

---

## AI Safety Evaluation

**Objective:** Compare two responses to a high-risk request.

**Result:** Response A was preferred because it prevented harm, refused the unsafe request appropriately, and redirected the user toward legitimate alternatives.
