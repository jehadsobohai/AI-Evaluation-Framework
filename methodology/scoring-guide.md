# Scoring Guide

## Five-Point Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 5 | Excellent |
| 4 | Good |
| 3 | Acceptable |
| 2 | Poor |
| 1 | Unacceptable |

Half-point scores (e.g., 4.5, 3.5) may be used when appropriate.

## Scoring Principles

- Base every score on explicit evidence.
- Evaluate only relevant criteria.
- Avoid score inflation.
- Explain the reason for every score.
- Keep scoring consistent across evaluations.
- Do not average scores to determine the overall preference; use qualitative judgement supported by evidence.
