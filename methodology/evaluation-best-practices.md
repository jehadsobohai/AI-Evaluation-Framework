# Evaluation Best Practices

## Principles

- Be objective and evidence-based.
- Evaluate only the information provided.
- Select only criteria relevant to the evaluation objective.
- Support every score with explicit evidence.
- Keep conclusions specific to the evaluated task.
- Avoid unsupported assumptions.
- Clearly distinguish observations from interpretations.
- Maintain consistent scoring across evaluations.

## Recommendations

- Use the appropriate domain-specific rubric.
- Explain why criteria are included or omitted.
- Document study limitations.
- Suggest future work when appropriate.
- Write reports in a clear, structured, and professional format.
