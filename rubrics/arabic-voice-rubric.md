# Arabic Voice Evaluation Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric is used to evaluate Arabic speech recordings based on pronunciation, speech quality, and listening experience.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Pronunciation Accuracy
- Speech Clarity
- Naturalness
- Prosody
- Intonation
- Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) Compliance (when applicable)
- Dialect Consistency (when applicable)
- Audio Quality
- Overall Listening Experience
- Tajweed Compliance (when applicable)

## Evaluation Guidance

- Evaluate only criteria relevant to the evaluation objective.
- Support every score with explicit evidence.
- Distinguish pronunciation quality from audio quality.
- Evaluate Tajweed only when the recording is a Qur'anic recitation.
- Base conclusions on qualitative analysis rather than score averages.
