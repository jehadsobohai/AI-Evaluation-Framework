# Arabic Language Evaluation Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric is used to evaluate the quality of Arabic text, with emphasis on Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), linguistic accuracy, and readability.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Grammar
- Spelling and Punctuation
- Fluency
- Naturalness
- Clarity
- Tone
- Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) Compliance
- Dialect Consistency (when applicable)
- Terminology (when applicable)

## Evaluation Guidance

- Evaluate only criteria relevant to the evaluation objective.
- Support every score with explicit evidence.
- Distinguish formal MSA from dialectal Arabic when appropriate.
- Base conclusions on qualitative analysis rather than score averages.
